## 🟣 About Me

I'm Gerald U. Ibina, currently studying **BSIT (Bachelor of Science in Industrial Technology)** at **Talisay City College**. I started my professional career at **17 years old** and now have **1 year and 11 months** of hands-on experience building websites for real clients. I hold a **National Certificate II in Computer System Servicing**, and I'm always striving to upskill — currently deepening my knowledge in full-stack development, CRO, and tracking setups.

I specialize in **custom WordPress and Shopify theme development**, and I also handle **CRO (conversion rate optimization)** and **CallRail call-tracking setup** for client sites. I make use of AI tools like **Claude and ChatGPT** to speed up my development workflow.
